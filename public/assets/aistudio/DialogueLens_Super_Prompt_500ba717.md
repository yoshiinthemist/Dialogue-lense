## Required behavior

### R01 — One owner for each decision

Establish one authority for each of these responsibilities: work eligibility and claims; playback intent and actual transport; acceptance and publication of content; committed bubble-body placement.

Workers execute or explicitly requeue their exact claimed task. A worker cannot independently publish a competing dialogue revision, issue a contradictory transport decision, or silently mark unsupported work complete. Migrate production callers to the relevant authority; remove or delegate superseded decision paths. Adding another manager without connecting existing callers is insufficient.

Apply eligibility and generation checks to required review claims too. Temporary contention defers eligible work with a wake path; obsolete generations are superseded or cancelled; unavailable capability and exhausted recovery reach explicit blocked or terminal outcomes, with a defined retry trigger where appropriate. A rejected claim cannot silently fall through to unclaimed review, become a false success, or loop forever merely to stay pending.

Publish and consume a coherent immutable presentation revision: accepted text, resolved speaker metadata, quality state, cue timing, and content identity must agree across overlay, transcript, saving, and relevant exports. Distinguish a logical utterance from its readable segments and from one playback activation. Revisions of an existing segment preserve its UI identity; distinct split segments get distinct keys and retain explicit parent/source-span lineage. Do not key identity only by current text, guessed speaker, timestamp bucket, or a fresh UUID on every acquisition.

Canonicalize again after source-based repair, rolling normalization, split/merge, and omitted-cue recovery. Those operations can create new overlaps even if the input was deduplicated. Reconcile window-spanning retained events by source lineage and actual overlap; deleting only events whose start lies inside the repair window is insufficient. Preserve user-protected fields and supported distinct utterances. A late response, higher model confidence, longer sentence, capitalization change, or prettier wording alone cannot establish replacement authority.

### R02 — Define what is ready and what is verified

Track these concepts separately:

| Concept | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence acquired | Original captions, audio, or frames are available with identity and timing. |
| Draft prepared | A candidate interpretation exists; it has not necessarily passed review. |
| Review completed | The required checks reached supported outcomes or an honest unresolved outcome. |
| Accepted/playable | A revision is approved for presentation at its declared quality level, including a permitted fallback. |
| Verified | The particular quality dimension was actually checked against appropriate evidence. |
| Unavailable | No usable result exists after bounded recovery, with the reason retained. |

Keep source wording, translation, speaker identity, timing, and presentation support separate. Available audio, a nonblank translation, upstream confidence, or two scouts agreeing cannot alone verify those dimensions.

Measure contiguous playable coverage and contiguous verified coverage independently from the viewer position. A gap interrupts the corresponding lead. A good cue farther ahead does not certify intervening media. Verified silence requires affirmative evidence; absent captions do not establish silence.

Define **verified dialogue lead** as continuous accepted intervals whose source wording and timing were checked against admissible original evidence, whose translation was checked when required, and whose speaker attribution is supported. A required unchecked dimension, uncertain-identity fallback, or missing interval stops that aggregate lead. Record the actual wording-check method: caption alignment must not be labeled independent audio verification. These distinctions must not prevent explicitly accepted fallback from contributing to playable lead. For non-speech intervals, verify the applicable sound/music content and timing without requiring a fictitious speaker or spoken-word check. Accepted sound-only coverage remains distinct from verified silence; speech-only checks cannot certify unresolved meaningful sound coverage. These different evidence states may legitimately share playable status when the applicable acceptance conditions are met.

`min(known video end, acknowledged viewer position + 180,000 ms)`
